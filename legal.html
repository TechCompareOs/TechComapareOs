About TechCompare OS

TechCompare OS is an online marketplace where verified Tshwane University of Technology (TUT) students can list and buy second-hand laptops, phones and tablets. The site is operated by [YOUR FULL NAME], a TUT student, in South Africa. We are a marketplace: the items are owned and described by the student sellers, not by us.

Terms of use

  • You must sign in with a valid TUT student email address (@tut4life.ac.za or @tut.ac.za). One person, one account.
  • Sellers must own the device or have the owner's permission to sell it. Stolen, counterfeit, blacklisted or financed-and-unpaid devices are not allowed and will be removed.
  • Listings must be honest: condition, faults, storage, battery health and what is included. Use your own photos.
  • A listing stays live for up to 30 days. Sellers can mark an item as sold or delete it at any time.
  • We may remove listings or suspend accounts that break these terms, mislead buyers or harass other users.
  • Do not share your sign-in. Messages on the site must stay respectful and relate to the sale.

Payments and seller payouts

  • Buyers pay on the site through PayFast (card, instant EFT and other methods PayFast offers). We never see or store your card details.
  • The payment is received by TechCompare OS, which holds it until the sale is complete.
  • A platform fee of 5% of the item price is deducted from the seller's share. Delivery fees, where charged, are paid by the buyer.
  • We pay the seller by EFT to the bank account they saved once the buyer confirms they received the item. If the buyer does not respond, the payout becomes due 7 days after payment, unless a dispute is open.

Delivery and collection

  • Courier delivery: the buyer chooses a courier option and pays the delivery fee at checkout. Delivery is arranged through The Courier Guy and collected from the seller's saved pickup address. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.
  • Collection from the seller: free. After payment, the buyer receives the seller's contact details to arrange a time. Meet in a safe public place on campus, check the device before confirming, and bring a friend where you can.
  • Sellers must pack the item securely and hand it to the courier promptly after payment.
  • Risk passes to the buyer once the buyer has the item. If a courier parcel is lost or damaged in transit, contact us within 48 hours so we can help claim from the courier.

Refunds, returns and disputes

  • Check the device as soon as you receive it. Only tap "I received my item" once you have inspected it and it matches the listing. That releases the seller's payment.
  • If the item is not as described (undisclosed fault, wrong model, missing parts) or does not arrive, email us before confirming receipt, and within 48 hours of delivery or collection. We will hold the seller's payout while we look into it.
  • If the seller's description was wrong, the buyer may return the item at the seller's cost and receive a refund of the item price. If the item never arrived, the buyer is refunded in full.
  • Refunds are paid back through the original payment method or by EFT, usually within 7 working days of the decision.
  • Buyers who simply change their mind after confirming receipt are not guaranteed a refund. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have under South African consumer law.

Privacy notice (POPIA)

We process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

  • What we collect: your TUT email address, name, cell number, delivery and pickup addresses, messages between buyers and sellers, order history, listing photos and details. Sellers also give bank account details so we can pay them.
  • Why: to verify you are a TUT student, run the marketplace, take payment, arrange couriers, pay sellers, and resolve disputes.
  • Who it is shared with: PayFast (payments), The Courier Guy (delivery, only the name, phone number and address needed for a parcel), and our hosting and database providers (Supabase, GitHub, Render). The seller's and buyer's contact details are shared with each other only for a paid order.
  • Bank details are visible only to the account owner and to the site operator for making payouts. They are never shown to other users.
  • How long: we keep order records for as long as needed for accounting, disputes and legal reasons, and delete or anonymise other data when it is no longer needed.
  • Your rights: you may ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing us. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
